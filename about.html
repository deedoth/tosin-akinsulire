About

Hi, I'm Tosin.

A healthcare business and commercial leader who believes business excellence should create impact beyond the numbers.

My story

Building stronger healthcare businesses and the people in them.

I have more than 12 years of experience across pharmacy, pharmaceutical sales and strategic healthcare marketing. My work brings together commercial strategy, business development, customer-centricity and strategic execution.

I care about building stronger healthcare businesses, developing people and contributing to a healthcare industry that creates real value for the communities it serves.

Organisations become stronger when commercial performance is supported by customer understanding, intentional leadership, capable people and excellent execution.

Purpose

To contribute to stronger healthcare organisations and more capable people through commercial excellence, strategic execution and purposeful leadership.

Vision

To be a recognised healthcare business leader whose contribution shapes organisational growth, leadership development and healthcare impact across Africa and beyond.

Mission

To apply strategic marketing, customer insight and intentional leadership to strengthen healthcare businesses, develop people and create lasting value.

Values

What I stand for.

Excellence

Meaningful results need high standards, intentional effort and continuous improvement. It shows in how I approach strategy, execution, collaboration and the quality of what I deliver.
